About Me

Exploring creativity through technology, art, and innovation.

Hi! I’m Minseok You, a multidisciplinary creator blending technology, design, and storytelling. My journey began in visual arts and animation, but it quickly expanded into data-driven creativity and digital innovation.

I’m currently studying a Bachelor of Information Technology (Data Science) at Macquarie University, where I actively contribute to student communities such as the Macquarie Aerospace Rover Society (MARS), Robotics Club (MURC), and the Association of Computing Students (MACS).

Alongside academics, I’m passionate about 3D animation, videography, and interactive media. My work often merges cinematic storytelling with real-time rendering tools like Blender and Unreal Engine 5.

I aim to build projects that connect engineering precision with artistic expression—whether it’s crafting an animated short, designing society merchandise, or experimenting with AI-driven visuals.
